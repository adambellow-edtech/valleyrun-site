Three moves. Endless swing.

Pump

Fold at the top

Fold as the marble turns and it climbs higher, just like pumping a swing. Open as it rolls back down.

Perfect

Hit the turn

Fold right as the marble turns for a Perfect. Perfects stack your multiplier and bring the band in.

Lip Stall

Stop on the edge

Let the marble coast to a stop right on the lip for triple points and a sun gem.

Your Songs

Swing to your own music

Add a song and Valley Run finds its beat, then builds a course where every gem lands on it. Hit a Perfect right on the beat and it scores double.

  • Any song you love. Search Apple Music, pick a file you own, or choose a song from your Music library.
  • Build it yourself. Place every gem on a timeline of the song, then play from any gem to hear it land.
  • Challenge friends. Share your course as a link. Friends bring their own copy of the song and play your exact layout.
  • Listen mode. Put music on a speaker and gems drop on whatever beat the microphone hears.

Made for iPhone Duo

The hinge is the controller

On iPhone Duo the horizon sits right on the fold, and folding the real hinge pumps the swing. Hold it like a book or stand it like a laptop.

On every other iPhone, tip the top edge up to pump and level it on the way down, in portrait or landscape.
