How it plays

Pump

Fold at the top

Fold the phone as the marble turns and it climbs higher, just like pumping a swing.

Perfect

Hit the beat

Fold right on the turn for a Perfect. Perfects stack your multiplier and bring in the soundtrack.

Lip Stall

Stop on the edge

Let the marble coast to a stop right on the lip for triple points and a sun gem.

Three worlds, thirty courses

World 1

Neon Coast

Learn the swing by the sea: chains, boost pads, drifting gems and the edge catch.

World 2

Glass Canyon

Smash glass with speed, bounce off springs and fight headwinds and mud.

World 3

Midnight Grid

Timed mines and laser fences. Nerve, timing and one marble to spare.

Made for iPhone Duo. Plays on every iPhone.

iPhone Duo

The real hinge is the controller. The horizon sits right on the fold.

Every other iPhone

Tilt the top edge of the screen up to fold, level it to open. Works in portrait and landscape.
