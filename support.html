Playing

How do I play?

A marble swings back and forth in a valley. Fold at the top of each swing and it climbs higher, like pumping a swing. Open again as it rolls down. Collect every gem before the clock or your marbles run out. Play First Swing from the title screen for a two-minute guided lesson.

I have an iPhone Duo. How do I control it?

Open the phone flat, then fold it a little to drop the marble. The hinge is the controller: folding makes the slopes steeper, opening makes them gentler. Hold it like a book or stand it like a laptop; the game follows.

I have a regular iPhone. How do I control it?

Tilt replaces the fold. Tip the top edge of the screen up (away from you) to fold, and level the phone to open. Whatever angle you hold it at rest counts as flat. If it feels off, pause and tap Recenter tilt.

What are Perfects, Lip Stalls and the Catch?

A Perfect is a fold right as the marble turns; Perfects raise your multiplier and bring in more of the music. A Lip Stall is stopping the marble right on the edge, worth triple points. If the marble flies off the edge, time slows: open quickly (or level the phone) to catch it, once per course.

The marble keeps flying off the edge.

When the ring ahead of the marble turns red, it is heading over. Fold more (or tip up more): a steeper slope stops it sooner.

How do I unlock more marbles?

Earn stars. Twelve marbles unlock at star milestones, from 5 stars up to all 90. Choose yours from Marbles on the map.

Where are my stats?

Tap the star count on the title screen. You'll see best score, fastest time and fewest folds for every course.

Game Center and clips

How do leaderboards work?

Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app. Valley Run posts your best score, fastest time and fewest folds per course, plus totals. Tap the trophy on the map to see them.

What is the screen recording permission for?

Valley Run can save a short clip of your best moment, like a Lip Stall or a three-star finish, so you can share it from the results card. Only the game screen is recorded, never the microphone, and the clip stays on your phone unless you share it. If you decline, the game works exactly the same without clips.

Your progress

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your iPhone, and it is included in your iPhone backups. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
